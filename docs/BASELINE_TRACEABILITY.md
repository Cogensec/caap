# CAAP v1.0 baseline traceability

The normative source used for this working repository is `CAAP v1.0.0-draft.1 - Public Review Draft`, dated 2026-07-16 in the source document.

Preserved elements include:

- the purpose and OWASP-beneath positioning;
- the 11 domain IDs, names, scopes, and primary alignments;
- the 25 public pattern IDs, titles, and baseline severity scores;
- the harmless-sentinel safety statement;
- the 14 required conformance record elements;
- the five result states;
- the six severity axes: impact, exploitability, privilege, autonomy, persistence, and propagation;
- the distinction between public conformance and reserved operational material.

CAAP-200 adds families, 175 pattern records, explicit relationships, maturity and implementation metadata, machine-readable schemas, a runner, adapter contracts, scoring, and contribution scaffolds. It does not convert private operational payloads, evasion methods, attack-chain mutation, production oracles, or customer-derived variants into public benchmark content.
