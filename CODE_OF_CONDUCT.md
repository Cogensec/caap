# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Participants must be respectful, assume good faith while testing claims, protect confidential information, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or unsafe security demonstrations.

Project spaces may not be used to target systems without authorization, distribute destructive agent payloads, disclose private vulnerability details, or pressure reviewers to weaken safety controls.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation to protect people and the project. Report conduct concerns privately to caap@cogensec.com. Reports will be handled discreetly and without retaliation.
